Why CivicReport exists

Reporting a broken streetlight should not take three office visits. CivicReport turns every resident into the eyes of the city: photograph the problem, let the AI handle the paperwork, and watch the neighbourhood repair itself, one report at a time.

Team CivicReport, Berhampur

How it works

From photo
to fixed.

The journey of a single report, from the moment you notice a problem to the moment it leaves the map.

01

Photograph it

Pothole, leak, dead streetlight or an overflowing bin. One photo is enough. The picture is resized in your browser before it is sent anywhere.

Takes about ten seconds

02

AI files the report

Gemini reads the photograph through this project's own server, names the issue, sets a category and severity, and drafts the description.

Key stays on the server

03

The community closes the loop

Neighbours confirm what they have seen, status changes land on the map immediately, and points are earned for useful contributions.
