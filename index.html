How it works

From photo
to fixed.

The journey of a single report — from the moment you notice a problem to the moment it disappears from the map.

01

Snap & upload

See a pothole, leak, dead streetlight or overflowing bin? Take a photo and drop it in. No forms, no queues, no paperwork.

Takes under 10 seconds

02

Gemini AI analyses the image, detects the issue type, assigns severity and drafts the description — the form fills itself.

Neighbours verify the report, authorities pick it up, status updates live on the map — and you earn points for the city.
